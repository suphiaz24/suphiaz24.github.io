Flapping-Wing Aerial-Aquatic Vehicle

BFL-01 · Bio-Inspired Fluid Lab · Undergraduate Researcher, advisor Dr. Sunghwan Jung · Sep 2026 – present

FabricationDesign stageCAD simulation assembly before fabrication

Purpose

In the lab’s bird design subgroup, I’m helping develop a flapping-wing aerial-aquatic vehicle modeled on MIT’s FAAV: a roughly 300 g robot, inspired by diving birds, that can both swim and fly (MIT News).

How it’s built

I built a Fusion 360 simulation assembly of the flapping-wing mechanism, a crank driving a gear and linkage, defining mates and joint constraints to model the wing kinematics and verify range of motion before anything is fabricated.
