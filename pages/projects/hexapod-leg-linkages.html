Hexapod Leg Linkages

CUPI-01 · For the Symbiote hexapod · Cornell University Physical Intelligence · Mechanical Design Team · Mar 2026 – present

FabricationDesign stageCAD redesign with material analysis

Purpose

Symbiote pairs an autonomous UAV quadcopter with a hexapod ground robot for coordinated terrain surveying. The hexapod has to carry sensor payloads across unstructured terrain.

Design

I redesigned the linkages for all 6 hexapod legs and reinforced the leg carriage in Fusion 360 and Onshape, working through iterative CAD and material analysis. The redesign increased range of motion and load capacity so the robot can support heavier sensor payloads.

The tactile foot sensor at the end of each leg is its own project: CUPI-02.
