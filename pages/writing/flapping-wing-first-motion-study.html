Flapping-wing drive: first motion study

LOG-01 · 17 Sep 2026 · Build log · Flapping-Wing Aerial-Aquatic Vehicle

I assembled the crank, gear, and linkage in Fusion 360 and ran a motion study to check the joint range before anything gets fabricated.

The assembly

The lab is reproducing MIT’s FAAV, a roughly 300 g diving-bird robot that both swims and flies. My piece is the wing drive: a crank driving a gear and linkage. I built it as a simulation assembly in Fusion 360, defining the mates and joint constraints.

Motion study

A motion study cycles the joints so I can check the range of motion of the wing drive before anything is fabricated.
