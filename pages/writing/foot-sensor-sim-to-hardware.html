Foot sensor: simulation first, then hardware

LOG-02 · 21 Sep 2026 · Build log · Tactile Foot Sensor

I proved out the foot sensor’s four pressure inputs in an Arduino simulation driving a live heatmap, then wired the first breadboard prototype and streamed real readings.

Simulating the inputs

Before wiring anything, I simulated the sensor’s four pressure inputs and an HX711 load-cell amplifier in an Arduino simulator. A Python script read the stream and drew a live foot-pressure heatmap with the centre of pressure, plus an estimate of the foot’s pitch and roll over time.

First wiring

Next came the first physical prototype on a breadboard: a RedBoard (Arduino-compatible), an HX711 amplifier, two load cells, and force-sensitive resistors.

Hardware test

With the hardware wired, the serial monitor streamed pitch, roll, pad compression, and raw FSR readings from the real sensors.
