Bio-Inspired Fluid Lab

Undergraduate Researcher · Ithaca, NY · Sep 2026 – present

Overview

I’m an undergraduate researcher in Dr. Sunghwan Jung’s Bio-Inspired Fluid Lab, working in the bird design subgroup.

What I did

  • Developing a flapping-wing aerial-aquatic vehicle modeled on MIT’s FAAV, a ~300 g diving-bird-inspired robot that both swims and flies, within the lab’s bird design subgroup.
  • Built a simulation assembly of the flapping-wing mechanism in Fusion 360, defining mates and joint constraints to model wing kinematics and verify range of motion before fabrication.
