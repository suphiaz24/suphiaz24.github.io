Team Bootleg

Mechanical Design Lead · Ithaca, NY · Nov 2025 – present

Overview

Bootleg is a side-project combat robotics team I’m on with a friend. It started with 6 people and is now a team of 4. I lead mechanical design on our 3 lb beetleweight, Nibble Newton, which competes in the National Havoc Robot League (NHRL).

What I did

  • Led mechanical design for a 4-person team building a 3 lb beetleweight combat robot, taking it from hand sketches to a full OnShape CAD model in 6 weeks and meeting the 3 lb weight limit with 1 oz to spare.
  • Iterated on the design through physical prototypes, testing each subsystem before committing to final hardware for the competition build.
  • Fabricated custom machined parts and carbon fiber composite layups and assembled all subsystems to tight tolerances; the robot survived 4 competition matches without structural failure.
