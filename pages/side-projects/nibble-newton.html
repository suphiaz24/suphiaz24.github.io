Nibble Newton

NHRL-01 · 3 lb beetleweight · Team Bootleg · National Havoc Robot League (NHRL) · Mechanical Design Lead · Nov 2025 – present

FabricationMachiningCarbon fiber composite layupAssembly to tight tolerances

Overview

Nibble Newton is a 3 lb beetleweight combat robot built by Bootleg, a side-project combat robotics team I’m on with a friend, to compete in the National Havoc Robot League (NHRL). The team started with 6 people and is now 4.

I led mechanical design for a 4-person team building a 3 lb beetleweight combat robot, taking it from hand sketches to a full OnShape CAD model in 6 weeks and meeting the 3 lb weight limit with 1 oz to spare.

Prototyping

We iterated through physical prototypes, testing each subsystem before committing to final hardware for the competition build.

Build & results

I fabricated custom machined parts and carbon fiber composite layups and assembled every subsystem to tight tolerances. The robot survived 4 competition matches without structural failure.
